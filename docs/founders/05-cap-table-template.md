## Key Cap Table Conventions

1. **Fully Diluted Basis** — All calculations include outstanding shares, options issued, and the entire option pool (whether granted or not)
2. **Vesting** — Founder shares subject to standard 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff (retroactive to incorporation date)
3. **409A Valuations** — Independent appraisal required annually and after each material event (funding round, significant revenue milestone)
4. **Information Rights** — Major investors (>5%) receive quarterly financials and annual audited statements
5. **Right of First Refusal (ROFR)** — Company holds ROFR on all secondary share transfers
6. **Co-Sale Rights** — Major investors have pro-rata co-sale rights on founder secondary sales
7. **Drag-Along** — Majority of Preferred stockholders can force sale of company on terms approved by board
8. **Board Composition (Post-Series A):** 5 seats — 2 Founder, 2 Series A investors, 1 independent
