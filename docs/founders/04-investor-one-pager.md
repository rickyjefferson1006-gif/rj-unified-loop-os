## The Problem

Enterprise AI is expensive and inefficient. Most agencies lock clients into a single AI provider — OpenAI or Anthropic — charging premium rates for every task regardless of complexity. Using GPT-5 to check inventory costs the same as using it to analyze a legal contract. That's architectural waste at scale.

| Vendor Lock-In | No knowledge transfer |
