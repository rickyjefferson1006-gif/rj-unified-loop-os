# Completion Report

## Status

COMPLETE or BLOCKED

## Delivered requirements

Map requirement IDs to files, behavior, and verification evidence.

## Evidence and decisions

Identify the primary evidence and consequential architecture decisions.

## Verification

List commands, environments, exit codes, coverage, security results, and manual checks.

## Deployment

Document build artifacts, configuration, secrets, migrations, health checks, rollout, and rollback.

## Operations

Document alerts, dashboards, SLOs, backups, recovery, support procedures, and ownership.

## Residual risk

List accepted risks, approver, expiration date, and remediation plan.

## Blocking items

List anything preventing completion. Never leave this empty when status is BLOCKED.
