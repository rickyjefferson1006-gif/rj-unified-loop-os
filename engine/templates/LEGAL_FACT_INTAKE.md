# Legal Fact Intake

Legal pages are generated from verified facts, not generic assumptions. Record the source and approver for each answer.

## Required business facts

- legal entity name, trade names, physical address, contact channels, and effective date;
- countries, states, and other jurisdictions served, targeted, or excluded;
- products, services, prices, subscriptions, trials, refunds, warranties, age limits, and regulated activities;
- account rules, acceptable use, user content, intellectual-property ownership, dispute process, governing law, and liability decisions;
- every personal-data field, collection source, purpose, legal basis where applicable, recipient, processor, retention period, storage location, and deletion path;
- cookies, SDKs, pixels, analytics, advertising, session replay, authentication, payment, CRM, email, SMS, phone, and support vendors;
- sale, sharing, targeted advertising, profiling, sensitive data, children, cross-border transfer, and consumer-rights facts;
- consent language, consent evidence, withdrawal, unsubscribe, opt-out, suppression, and preference-center operations;
- copyright-agent designation status, takedown contact and process, counter-notice process, and repeat-infringer policy;
- accessibility standard, support method, audit status, and remediation owner.

## Gate

Each policy must list applicability, supporting facts, owner, reviewer, effective date, version, unresolved questions, and operational controls. No unresolved placeholder may ship. Regulated, disputed, or high-risk language stays blocked until the configured qualified legal reviewer approves it.
