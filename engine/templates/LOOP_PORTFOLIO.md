# RJ Loop OS Portfolio

RJ Loop OS contains 45 governed loops. Use one loop for a bounded outcome or chain loops through their declared `handoff_to` targets.

| Family | Loops | Primary outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Research and product | Research Intelligence, Product PRD, Architecture and Threat Model, Market Opportunity | Evidence becomes an approved build or investment decision |
| Software delivery | Full Software Build, Test and Repair, Release and Deployment, Dependency Maintenance | Reproducible, tested, secure releases |
| Reliability and infrastructure | Service Monitoring, Incident Response, Backup and Disaster Recovery, Cost Optimization | Available, recoverable, cost-controlled services |
| Digital experience | Website/App/CRM/Search Experience, SEO/GEO/AEO/AIO Content Authority | Complete digital properties with search and conversion systems |
| AI systems | Model Routing, Agent Evaluation, RAG and Knowledge, Memory Governance, Prompt Optimization | Measured agents with governed knowledge, memory, safety, and cost |
| Credit operations | Client Onboarding, Credit Report Audit, Credit Dispute Case, Complaint Escalation, Score Analytics | Authorized, individualized, evidence-backed case operations |
| Compliance and privacy | Compliance Audit, Privacy and Data Rights, Forms and Consent, Document and Policy Governance, Vendor Risk | Traceable requirements, controls, reviews, and remediation |
| Business finance | Business Credit Progression, Funding Readiness, Grant and Contract Readiness, Billing and Reconciliation | Readiness analysis, authorized submissions, and reconciled money records |
| Education | Financial Education Course | Researched, accessible, assessed, maintained learning products |
| Growth | Offer and Pricing, Conversion Funnel, Lead Nurture, Sales Pipeline, Content Distribution, Reputation, Affiliate and Partner | Compliant acquisition, conversion, retention, and partnerships |
| Client and data operations | Client Delivery, CRM Data Quality, Client Support, Analytics and Experiment | Reliable delivery, clean records, support resolution, and measured improvement |

## Recommended master chains

### New software or SaaS product

`research-intelligence` → `market-opportunity` → `product-prd` → `architecture-threat-model` → `full-software-build` → `website-experience-build` → `release-deployment` → `service-monitoring`

### Credit-service client

`client-onboarding` → `credit-report-audit` → `credit-dispute-case` → `client-delivery` → `credit-score-analytics`

Use `complaint-escalation` only when documented facts and qualified review establish that escalation is appropriate.

### Business credit and funding client

`client-onboarding` → `business-credit-progression` → `funding-readiness` → `client-delivery` → `billing-reconciliation`

The readiness loop never guarantees approval and cannot submit an application without client approval.

### Authority and acquisition system

`market-opportunity` → `offer-pricing` → `seo-content-authority` → `content-distribution` → `conversion-funnel` → `lead-nurture` → `sales-pipeline` → `client-onboarding`

### AI agent production system

`research-intelligence` → `rag-knowledge` → `memory-governance` → `prompt-optimization` → `ai-model-routing` → `agent-evaluation` → `release-deployment` → `service-monitoring`

## Operating rule

Event-driven loops respond to a verified event. Scheduled loops review drift and risk. Human-approval steps are not automated away. High-risk and critical loops require both their named approvals and the final release approval before completion.
