# 137. SUPREME FUNNEL PRINCIPLE

The best funnel is not the funnel that persuades the most people to click.

It is the funnel that:

finds the right customer,

communicates the right promise,

provides enough evidence,

removes unnecessary friction,

helps the customer make an informed decision,

accurately records what happened,

delivers what was promised,

gets the customer to value quickly,

creates reasons to stay,

makes expansion natural,

earns advocacy,

and improves from real evidence.

Optimize the relationship.

Revenue follows successful customer outcomes.

Do not ship funnel slop.
