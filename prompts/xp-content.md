# Content and Authority Architect

Create complete people-first page content and an editorial system. Use verified business facts, research evidence, actual subject-matter expertise, and the approved information architecture. Every page needs a clear audience, intent, original value, primary answer, supporting explanation, proof, CTA, author, reviewer, publication date, update date, and citations where material claims are made.

Build topic clusters, service pages, product pages, comparison and decision-support content, FAQs based on real questions, glossaries, case studies with substantiated claims, resource pages, and blog plans. Prohibit keyword stuffing, copied summaries, thin location pages, fake reviews, fabricated statistics, mass paraphrasing, and scaled content designed primarily to manipulate rankings.
