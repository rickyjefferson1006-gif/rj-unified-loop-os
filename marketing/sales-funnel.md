# RJ Business Solutions — Sales Funnel Architecture

## Funnel Overview

```
Traffic → Lead Magnet → Landing Page → Email Nurture → Consultation → Proposal → Close → Onboarding → Retention
```

---

## Stage 1: Traffic Sources

| Channel | Content Type | Target |
|:--|:--|:--|
| SEO/Blog | "Why Multi-Model AI Beats Single-Provider", "60% AI Cost Savings" | CTOs, VP Engineering |
| LinkedIn | Thought leadership, case study highlights | Enterprise decision-makers |
| Twitter/X | Model comparison threads, ROI stats | Technical founders |
| Paid Ads | "Cut AI Costs by 60%" — Meta, LinkedIn, Google | VP-level buyers |
| GitHub | Open-source agent templates, routing examples | Developer champions |

---

## Stage 2: Lead Magnet — ROI Calculator

**Headline:** "How Much Is Single-Model AI Costing You?"

**Offer:** Free interactive calculator showing projected savings with multi-model routing.

**Capture:** Name, company email, monthly AI spend, current provider.

**Immediate Value:** Personalized savings projection delivered on the thank-you page + emailed as PDF.

---

## Stage 3: Landing Page (13 Sections)

See `landing-page-spec.md` for the full 13-section specification:

1. Sticky nav (brand + Book Call CTA)
2. Hero (kicker + gradient headline + 4-stat row)
3. Trust logos
4. Problem section (5 pain cards)
5. Solution section (5 feature cards)
6. Services grid (12 cards)
7. Metrics (4 big-number stats)
8. Testimonial hero
9. Pricing (3-tier, middle featured)
10. FAQ (2-col accordion)
11. Final CTA (full-bleed gradient)
12. Footer (4-col with brand block)

**Key conversion elements:**
- Hero CTA: "Schedule Free Consultation"
- Pricing CTAs: "Get Quote" / "Schedule Enterprise Demo"
- ROI Calculator embedded inline
- Case study social proof throughout

---

## Stage 4: Email Nurture Sequence

See `email-sequences.md` for the full 5-email cold outreach sequence:

1. **The Problem:** "Is your AI overspending by 60%?"
2. **The Solution:** Model routing architecture
3. **Proof:** E-commerce case study ($200K savings)
4. **Objection Handling:** "We already use ChatGPT"
5. **Clear CTA:** 15-minute call

**Timing:** Days 1, 3, 5, 8, 12 after lead capture.

---

## Stage 5: Consultation Booking

**Method:** Calendly-style scheduling embedded on site.

**Pre-call:** Auto-send a brief prep questionnaire:
- Current AI providers and monthly spend
- Top 3 automation use cases
- Team size and technical maturity

**Call structure (30 min):**
1. (5 min) Discovery — understand current stack
2. (10 min) Live demo — routing simulator with their numbers
3. (10 min) Solution fit — which tier matches their needs
4. (5 min) Next steps — proposal timeline

---

## Stage 6: Proposal & Pitch

**Deliverables:**
- Custom 10-slide pitch deck (see `docs/founders/02-pitch-deck-outline.md`)
- Technical architecture diagram showing model routing for their use case
- ROI projection with their actual numbers
- Project timeline (4-8 weeks for Starter, 8-16 for Professional, 16-24 for Enterprise)

**Pricing presentation:**
- Show the 3-tier structure with their recommended tier highlighted
- Include the savings projection vs their current spend
- Milestone payment terms: 30% / 40% / 30%

---

## Stage 7: Close

**Payment terms:**
- 30% upon contract signing
- 40% at development completion
- 30% upon production deployment

**Risk reversal:**
- Money-back guarantee if production-ready agent not delivered on time

**Contract package:**
- Statement of Work with milestones
- Service Level Agreement
- NDA (if not already signed)

---

## Stage 8: Onboarding

**Week 1:**
- Kickoff call with technical team
- System access and API key provisioning
- Knowledge base and documentation intake
- Sandbox environment setup

**Deliverables:**
- Client portal access (branded dashboard)
- Project tracker with milestones
- Weekly status call schedule

---

## Stage 9: Retention & Expansion

**Monthly:**
- Performance reports (tokens saved, cost avoided, resolution rates)
- Model update recommendations
- New feature availability notices

**Quarterly:**
- Optimization review
- ROI tracking against baseline
- Expansion opportunity analysis
- Same-day new model integration

**Referral program:**
- 10% referral fee for new client introductions
- Co-branded case study opportunities

---

## Conversion Rate Benchmarks

| Stage | Target Conversion |
|:--|:--|
| Visitor → Lead (ROI Calculator) | 3-5% |
| Lead → Consultation Booked | 15-20% |
| Consultation → Proposal Requested | 50-60% |
| Proposal → Close | 30-40% |
| Overall: Visitor → Customer | 0.2-0.5% |
