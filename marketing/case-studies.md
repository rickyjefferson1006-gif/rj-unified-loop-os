## Case Study 1: E-commerce Customer Support

**Company:** MidSize Retail Co.
**Industry:** E-commerce
**Revenue:** $50M
**Location:** United States + China

### The Challenge
- 10,000+ monthly support tickets overwhelming 5-person team
- Average response time: 4 hours (customer frustration)
- English + Chinese support needed (translation costs high)
- Using GPT-4 for all queries: $4,500/month AI costs
- Ticket resolution rate: only 65%
- Customer satisfaction: 72%

### Our Solution
Deployed a multi-agent customer support system with intelligent model routing:

**Model Architecture:**
- **Triage Agent:** Llama 3.3 70B with NVIDIA NIM → $200/month
  - Categorizes and prioritizes incoming tickets
  - Routes to appropriate specialist agent
  - 95% cheaper than GPT-5 for this simple task
  
- **Standard Response Agent:** Claude Sonnet 4.6 → $800/month
  - Handles 70% of customer queries
  - Balanced quality and cost
  
- **Complex Issues Agent:** Claude Opus 4.7 → $400/month
  - Deep reasoning for difficult problems
  - Escalation handling
  
- **Chinese Support Agent:** GLM-5-2 → $300/month
  - Native Chinese language understanding (not translation)
  - 75% cheaper than using GPT-5 for Chinese
  
**Total AI Cost:** $1,700/month (vs $4,500 with GPT-4 only = **62% savings**)

### Results
- ✅ **75% ticket auto-resolution** (vs 65% before)
- ✅ **<1 minute average response time** (vs 4 hours)
- ✅ **$200,000 annual savings** (reduced staffing + AI costs)
- ✅ **89% customer satisfaction** (vs 72%)
- ✅ **Native Chinese support** without translation costs

## Case Study 3: Global SaaS Sales Automation

**Company:** B2B SaaS Platform
**Industry:** Software
**Markets:** United States, China, Europe
**Team:** 10 sales reps (5 English, 3 Chinese, 2 European)

- Multiple languages requiring expensive specialists
- GPT-4 for all markets: $6,500/month AI costs
- English-only AI struggling with Chinese/European leads
- Slow response time losing deals

### Our Solution
Geographic sales automation with region-specific model routing:

**Model Architecture:**
- **English Leads:** GPT-5.2 analysis → $1,800/month
  - Lead scoring and qualification
